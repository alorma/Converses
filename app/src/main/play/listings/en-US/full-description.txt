Converses helps you remember your conversations with the people in your life: what you told them, what they told you, and what happened between you. Whether you talk on a chat app, on social networks, by phone or in person, everything stays in one private timeline per person.

ONE TIMELINE PER PERSON
• Each note is one of three kinds: something you said, something they said, or something that happened.
• Record where it happened: chat, social network, phone or in person.
• Filter the timeline by kind to find what you need.
• Pin key facts about each person so they're always at hand.

QUICK TO CAPTURE
• Add a quick note in seconds, right after a conversation.
• Set when it happened, even if it was days ago.
• Edit or delete any entry, with undo if you change your mind.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your notes and the people in them stay on your device and your paired watch: they never go online. No accounts, no ads and no tracking.
• Protect sensitive people with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Their notes stay hidden until you unlock them, and screenshots are blocked.
• Your notes are excluded from cloud backups.
• Export a person's notes as plain text whenever you want, and share them with the app you choose.

ON YOUR WRIST (WEAR OS)
• Browse your people and their latest notes from your watch.
• Dictate a note by voice. If your phone is out of reach, it waits and is delivered as soon as it's back.
• Protected people open on the watch only after you approve it with your fingerprint on the phone.
• Needs the Converses phone app, and nothing goes through the cloud.

KEEP IT TIDY
• Archive people you no longer talk to, without losing their history.
• Light and dark themes, plus dynamic color that matches your wallpaper.

Converses is for anyone who wants to be a better friend, partner, colleague or listener: remember the details, follow up on what matters, and never lose track of what was said.
