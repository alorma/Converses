Version 1.7.6:
• Converses now has a Wear OS app. Your people and their latest notes on your wrist, where you were going to look at your phone anyway.
• Dictate a note on the watch and it lands on your phone. Misheard? Dictate it again.
• Protected people open on the watch only after you approve it with your fingerprint on the phone.
• Missing the app on your watch or phone? Converses notices and offers to install it.
